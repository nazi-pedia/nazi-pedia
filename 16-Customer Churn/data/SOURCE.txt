Telco-Customer-Churn.csv

This table is the IBM telco customer churn sample distributed in the
repository IBM/telco-customer-churn-on-icp4d, at data/Telco-Customer-Churn.csv.
That repository is under the Apache License 2.0. A copy of the license is
data/LICENSE.

The earlier Watson Analytics filename was WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn.csv.

The file has 7043 customers and 21 columns. The label Churn is Yes or No.
